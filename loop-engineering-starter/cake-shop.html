Our Story

Sweet Layers started in 2019 as a home bakery on a single oven and a dream. Today we operate a full kitchen in Brooklyn, crafting cakes for birthdays, weddings, and every quiet Tuesday in between. Everything is made from scratch using locally sourced butter, free-range eggs, and real vanilla — because the best ingredients make the best memories.
